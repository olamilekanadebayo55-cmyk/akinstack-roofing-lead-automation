# Roofing Lead Generation & AI Voice Outreach with n8n

A practical automation system built with n8n for discovering roofing companies, filtering leads, researching prospects, generating personalized outreach, and managing AI voice calls.

## Workflow

The system is designed around this process:

Google Maps / Apify
↓
Lead Filtering
↓
Deduplication
↓
Google Sheets
↓
Competitor Research
↓
AI Personalization
↓
AI Voice Outreach
↓
Outcome Classification
↓
Lead Update

## Technologies

- n8n
- Apify
- Google Maps data
- Google Sheets
- SerpAPI
- OpenAI
- Vapi

## Workflows

### Lead Discovery and Filtering

Discovers potential roofing leads, filters them, removes duplicates, and stores qualified leads.

### AI Voice Outreach

Processes eligible leads, performs research, generates personalized messaging, initiates AI voice outreach, classifies outcomes, and updates the lead record.

## Important

The workflow files in this repository are sanitized examples.

Before using them, replace the placeholder credentials and IDs with your own n8n credentials and configuration.

Do not publish API keys, passwords, private customer information, or other sensitive credentials.
